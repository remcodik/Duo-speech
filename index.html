🤖 AI coach

The AI plays the characters and coaches your grammar. It runs on your own Claude API key — you pay Anthropic directly, and this app shows every cent. Without a key the island still works with scripted characters (free, simpler feedback).

Stored only in this browser (localStorage), sent only to api.anthropic.com. Tip: create a dedicated key with a spend limit at console.anthropic.com — you can revoke it anytime.

AI calls stop when the session total reaches the cap. Scripted mode keeps working.

🎙️ Voice & privacy

Heads-up: in some browsers (e.g. Chrome) speech recognition sends your audio to the browser vendor's servers to transcribe. In AI mode your text is sent to Anthropic. Prefer full privacy? Type your answers and play scripted mode — nothing leaves your device.
